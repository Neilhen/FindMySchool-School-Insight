Methodology and sources

One rule governs everything here: a figure is published only if it can be traced to a named source, and shown with a link to it. Where a source is silent, the site says so instead of estimating.

Where each figure comes from

WhatSourceNotes
Every school, its roll number, address, ethos, gender, enrolment, patron, language of instruction, DEIS status Department of Education school register (ArcGIS open data), CC BY 4.0 4,202 schools carry a named verification source. Updated annually; enrolment is the Department's most recent published year, stated on each page.
Preschools and early-years settings Tusla Early Years register A statutory register of providers. It carries a name and address and little else, so these pages are deliberately sparse — and the sparsest are marked noindex rather than padded out.
Special classes — how many, what type, which are new NCSE open data 1,995 schools. Datasets are matched by title, so a new school year is picked up without a code change. A sanctioned class is not a free place: availability changes through the year and the NCSE's local SENO is the authority.
Oversubscription, places, application deadlines Each school's own Annual Admission Notice 537 notices found and read, 89 yielding a ratio we are willing to publish. See below — this is where we are strictest.
Fees Each fee-paying school's own website Published only with the year and a link to the page it was read from. 17 of 59 fee-paying schools have a figure we could verify; the rest show none. That is a gap in our reading, not evidence the school is free.
Local house prices CSO PxStat HPA04, median residential sale price by Eircode routing key Joined on the school's own Eircode district. It describes the area, not the school, and a routing key can be large and mixed.
Area deprivation Pobal HP Deprivation Index 2022 Measured at Electoral Division level. It describes the population of an area, says nothing about a school's teaching, and should not be read as a quality score.
School transport eligibility Department of Education scheme rules Computed from straight-line distance to the nearest school of the right type: 3.2 km primary, 4.8 km post-primary. An indication, not a decision — Bus Éireann decides, and children in special classes travel under a separate scheme that these distances do not govern.

How the admission figures are read, and what we refuse to publish

These are the numbers most likely to change a decision, so they get the most scepticism. A wrong ratio is worse than no ratio: it could talk a family out of a school their child would have walked into.

  • Like for like, or labelled.A ratio divides one year's applications by that same year's places. Where a notice gives applications but no matching places figure, the comparison crosses two school years and the page says so in those words.
  • The intake, not the school.Primary notices report a school-wide total and then break it down. Dividing a whole-school application count by a Junior Infants place count once produced 10.4 applicants per place at a school whose real figure was 2.9. We now pair the places and applications that sit together in one breakdown block, and show the school-wide total separately where it differs.
  • The year is named.Some notices report on a year two or three back. The page names the year rather than saying "last year".
  • Sub-intakes are rejected.A special class of three seats is not the mainstream intake. Ratios built on implausibly small denominators are discarded rather than published.
  • Unreadable means unpublished.Where the notice cannot be parsed with confidence, we publish the link and no number, and say in plain words how confident the reading is.

Every school with a notice links to the document itself, so you can check our arithmetic against the source.

What we deliberately do not do

  • No league table and no school ratings.No official body publishes a quality ranking of Irish schools, and constructing one from enrolment, fees or area deprivation would be inventing a measurement rather than reporting one.
  • No catchment boundaries.Because none exist in law. Planning areas are shown as what they are: a Department planning instrument.
  • No paid placement.No school can pay to rank higher, be featured, or have information removed.
  • No estimated figures.A blank means we could not verify it. It never means zero.

Known limits

  • Admission-notice coverage is partial. Schools publish them on their own websites in their own formats, some as scanned images we cannot read.
  • Enrolment is the Department's most recent published year and will lag the current roll.
  • 296 schools have no county in the source data; theirs is taken from the nearest school that has one.
  • House prices and deprivation describe an area, not a school.

Found something wrong? Corrections are welcome and a correction from the school itself outranks anything we inferred.

Where this comes from. School details are from the Department of Education's schools register (CC BY 4.0). Special-class provision is from the National Council for Special Education's open data. Area deprivation is the Pobal HP Deprivation Index 2022. House prices are CSO median residential sale prices by Eircode district. Fees are read from each school's own website and carry a link to the page they came from.

What this is not. Ireland has no school catchment areas. Under the Education (Admission to Schools) Act 2018 every school sets its own oversubscription criteria and a child may apply to any school in the country. Nothing here tells you whether a child will get a place.
