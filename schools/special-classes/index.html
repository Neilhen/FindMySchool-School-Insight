Schools with special classes in Ireland

Every school in Ireland running an NCSE-sanctioned special class of any type. Special classes cover autism, ASD early intervention, specific speech and language disorder, hearing impairment, and mild, moderate and severe general learning disability.

A sanctioned class is not a free place. Ring the school and speak to your NCSE Special Educational Needs Organiser, who knows what is open this year.

What this is not. Ireland has no school catchment areas. Under the Education (Admission to Schools) Act 2018 every school sets its own oversubscription criteria and a child may apply to any school in the country. Nothing here tells you whether a child will get a place.
