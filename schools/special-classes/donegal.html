Schools with special classes in County Donegal

Every school in County Donegal running an NCSE-sanctioned special class of any type. Special classes cover autism, ASD early intervention, specific speech and language disorder, hearing impairment, and mild, moderate and severe general learning disability.

A sanctioned class is not a free place. Ring the school and speak to your NCSE Special Educational Needs Organiser, who knows what is open this year.
