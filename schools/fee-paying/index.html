Fee-paying schools in Ireland

Schools in Ireland that charge tuition. Most Irish schools do not: the State pays teachers' salaries in the free scheme, and a fee-paying school charges on top of that for smaller classes, facilities or boarding.

We publish a fee figure only where we could read it on the school's own website and link to the page it came from. Most schools here therefore show no figure — that means we could not verify one, not that the school is cheap. Boarding, transition year, books and uniforms are usually extra and are not included.
