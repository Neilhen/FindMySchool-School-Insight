DEIS Urban Band 2 schools in Ireland

Urban Band 2 schools receive extra staffing, a Home School Community Liaison coordinator, School Completion Programme access and enhanced capitation. Band 2 does not carry the reduced class sizes that Band 1 does — that is the main practical difference between the two. What each band gets →

What this is not. Ireland has no school catchment areas. Under the Education (Admission to Schools) Act 2018 every school sets its own oversubscription criteria and a child may apply to any school in the country. Nothing here tells you whether a child will get a place.
