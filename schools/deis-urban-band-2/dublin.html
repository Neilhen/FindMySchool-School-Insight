DEIS Urban Band 2 schools in County Dublin

Urban Band 2 schools receive extra staffing, a Home School Community Liaison coordinator, School Completion Programme access and enhanced capitation. Band 2 does not carry the reduced class sizes that Band 1 does — that is the main practical difference between the two. What each band gets →
