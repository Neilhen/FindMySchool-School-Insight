Where this comes from. School details are from the Department of Education's schools register (CC BY 4.0). Special-class provision is from the National Council for Special Education's open data. What this is not. Ireland has no school catchment areas. Under the Education (Admission to Schools) Act 2018 every school sets its own oversubscription criteria and a child may apply to any school in the country. Nothing here tells you whether a child will get a place.
