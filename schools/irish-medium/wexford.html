Gaelscoileanna and Irish-medium schools in County Wexford

Schools in County Wexford that teach through Irish. Two different things appear here and the difference matters: a gaelscoil or gaelcholáiste teaches everything through Irish, while an Irish unit is a stream inside an English-medium school. Each school below says which it is.

Schools in a Gaeltacht area are flagged on their own page. Being in the Gaeltacht and being Irish-medium are not the same thing — some Gaeltacht schools teach through English.

What this is not. Ireland has no school catchment areas. Under the Education (Admission to Schools) Act 2018 every school sets its own oversubscription criteria and a child may apply to any school in the country. Nothing here tells you whether a child will get a place.
