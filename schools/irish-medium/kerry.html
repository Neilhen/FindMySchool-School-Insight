Gaelscoileanna and Irish-medium schools in County Kerry

Schools in County Kerry that teach through Irish. Two different things appear here and the difference matters: a gaelscoil or gaelcholáiste teaches everything through Irish, while an Irish unit is a stream inside an English-medium school. Each school below says which it is.

Schools in a Gaeltacht area are flagged on their own page. Being in the Gaeltacht and being Irish-medium are not the same thing — some Gaeltacht schools teach through English.
