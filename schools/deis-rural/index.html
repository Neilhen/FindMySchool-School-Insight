Where this comes from. School details are from the Department of Education's schools register (CC BY 4.0). Special-class provision is from the National Council for Special Education's open data. Area deprivation is the Pobal HP Deprivation Index 2022. House prices are CSO median residential sale prices by Eircode district. Fees are read from each school's own website and carry a link to the page they came from.

What this is not. Ireland has no school catchment areas. Under the Education (Admission to Schools) Act 2018 every school sets its own oversubscription criteria and a child may apply to any school in the country. Nothing here tells you whether a child will get a place.
