  • Fees with provenance. Where a fee-paying school publishes a figure, it appears with the school year and a link to the page it was read from. Where one does not, we say so instead of guessing.

One thing worth being clear about

Ireland has no school catchment areas. Under the Education (Admission to Schools) Act 2018 every school sets its own oversubscription criteria and a child may apply to any school in the country. Nothing on this site tells you whether a child will get a place — for that you need the school's own admissions policy. What the map shows is the Department's planning areas, which govern where capacity gets built.
